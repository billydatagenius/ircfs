Compliance Questions, Answered

Does my company need audited financial statements?

Not always. The Companies Act uses a Public Interest Score (based on turnover, staff, third-party funding and employee-owner counts). Low-score private companies may only need a compliant AFS or an independent review — we assess your score and recommend the right path.

What happens if I miss a CIPC annual return?

CIPC charges escalating late-filing penalties, and if annual returns stay unfiled the company can be deregistered — which exposes directors personally to the company's debts. We restore companies in business rescue-style clean-ups and keep future returns current.

When must my business register for VAT?

Registration is mandatory once taxable turnover exceeds R1 million in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is possible from R50,000 if it benefits your cash flow. We prepare the registration and first submissions.

Do you handle SARS verification letters and queries?

Yes. We prepare and submit verification responses, dispute notices and suspensions of collection measures on your behalf — you never face a SARS letter alone.

Can you take over compliance from another accountant?

Regularly. We start with a full health check of past filings, reconcile any gaps with CIPC and SARS, then migrate the compliance calendar — usually without disturbing your current operations at all.
